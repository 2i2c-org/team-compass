# Cost reduction plan and framework

This is how 2i2c decides when we need to reduce costs and what to cut.
It covers both short-term and long-term cost cutting.

{button}`Cost reduction plan <https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zgNxdWVNDbcY-J34bhjavI1fbSmPrv5MJO3HdNb1ED4/edit?usp=sharing>`

## When to use it

Use this plan when our [financial health indicators](#finance:financial-health-indicators) suggest that both our runway is tight and cost coverage is low.

To see how a cut would change our runway, use [What if costs changed?](https://finance.2i2c.org/projections/#what-if-costs-changed) on the finance dashboard.
